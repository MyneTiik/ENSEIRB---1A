# 4) Tableaux des Primitives Usuelles

## A) Fonctions usuelles et puissances
* $\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \quad (n \neq -1)$
* $\int \frac{1}{x} \, dx = \ln(|x|) + C$
* $\int e^x \, dx = e^x + C$
* $\int a^x \, dx = \frac{a^x}{\ln(a)} + C$

## B) Fonctions trigonométriques
* $\int \cos(x) \, dx = \sin(x) + C$
* $\int \sin(x) \, dx = -\cos(x) + C$
* $\int \frac{1}{\cos^2(x)} \, dx = 1 + \tan^2(x) \dots = \tan(x) + C$  *(ou $\operatorname{tg}(x)$)*
* $\int \frac{1}{\sin^2(x)} \, dx = -\cotan(x) + C$

## C) Fonctions hyperboliques
* $\int \operatorname{ch}(x) \, dx = \operatorname{sh}(x) + C$
* $\int \operatorname{sh}(x) \, dx = \operatorname{ch}(x) + C$

## D) Fonctions trigonométriques inverses (Arrc) et Hyperboliques inverses (Arg)
* $\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \, dx = \arcsin(x) + C$
* $\int \frac{-1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \, dx = \arccos(x) + C$
* $\int \frac{1}{1 + x^2} \, dx = \arctan(x) + C$
* $\int \frac{1}{1 - x^2} \, dx = \operatorname{arg th}(x) + C$  *(ou $\operatorname{arg coth}(x)$ selon domaine)*
* $\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + 1}} \, dx = \operatorname{arg sh}(x) + C$
* $\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} \, dx = \operatorname{arg ch}(x) + C$
